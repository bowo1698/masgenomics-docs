The meiosis diagram numbers replication as step 1, homologous pairing as
step 2, crossing over as step 3, meiosis I as step 4, and meiosis II as
step 5. The incomplete-dominance diagram depicts an `RR × rr` cross,
not an `Rr × Rr` cross; the page explains its relationship to the
`C^R` and `C^W` notation in the accompanying example.
